# BUG-003 - Missing Terrace Floor Geometry

## Description

A section of floor geometry is missing on the underside of a house terrace, allowing the environment behind the floor to be visible.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Launch build V.0.0.4.0.
2. Locate the house with the accessible terrace.
3. Move the character to a position below the terrace.
4. Look toward the underside of the terrace.
5. Observe the floor geometry.

## Expected Result

The terrace floor should have complete geometry, with no visible gaps or missing sections when viewed from below.

## Actual Result

A section of the terrace floor geometry is missing when viewed from below, allowing the environment behind the floor to be visible.

## Repro Rate

100%

## Severity

Medium
