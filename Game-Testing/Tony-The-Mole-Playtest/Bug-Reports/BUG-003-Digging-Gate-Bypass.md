### **BUG-003: Digging Spot Allows Player to Bypass Gate and Access Locked Area**



##### **Description**



A specific digging spot near the gate allows the player to pass through the gate and access an area that is intended to remain locked.



##### **Steps to Reproduce**



1. Launch build 24305160.
2. Access the gated section.
3. Move towards the left side of the gate.
4. Hold the Dig input until the character starts digging.
5. Move forward while releasing the Dig input.



##### **Expected Result**



The player should not be able to use the digging mechanic to pass through the gate before the area is unlocked.



##### **Actual Result**



The player can pass through the gate and access the locked area before it is unlocked.



##### **Repro Rate**



5/5 attempts



##### **Severity**



Medium
